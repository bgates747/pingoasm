# Pingo Terrain development log — 2026-08-15

## Regular-LOD presentation control revised

The Author rejected the attitude-derived false horizon as a stand-in for
missing terrain. FSIM entry `R` now composites Pingo's transparent target over
one full-frame Agon palette-index 47 sky clear. The prior ground-colour wedge,
horizon calculation, and pitch/roll dependency are absent from this fixture;
the earlier flight fixtures retain their historical backdrop for comparison.

The Marina start remains in cell `(80,125)` at local position
`(4275,8000,466)`, but its initial camera rotation is now level with zero pitch
and roll and yaw `-8192`, facing almost due north.

The same fixture also sends the existing Pingo command-46 runtime switch to
disable scene illumination. Terrain remains in flat-pattern mode so no corpus
or mesh ABI changes, but unity illumination selects the accepted solid base
patterns only: palette index 20 for water, 7 for developed land, and 21 for
other land. No illumination-dependent dither band is selected. The stored
directional and ambient settings remain inert, permitting a later runtime
comparison without rebuilding terrain.

A possible later presentation experiment is to bake real cast-shadow
information with Blender's renderer into terrain classifications or auxiliary
face data. That would be an offline derivative of the accepted geometry, with
the unlit three-colour rendering retained as the diagnostic control. It is not
part of this revision: the immediate purpose is to inspect terrain shape and
semantic coverage without dither or runtime-lighting ambiguity.

The Author accepted this as a minor presentation change after visual review.
The revised appliance, generated listing/symbols, build receipt, source-level
contracts, and complete 435-test host result are preserved together.
