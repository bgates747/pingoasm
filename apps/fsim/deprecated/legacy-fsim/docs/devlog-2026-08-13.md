# Pingo Terrain development log — 2026-08-13

## Natural-terrain near-field palette reduced to three classes

The Author's near-field review clarified that the previously accepted natural
master was accepted at regional/orbital scale, not at roughly 100 m viewing
distance. At near range its many semantic colors, irregular triangles, and
thin constraint slivers read as "dazzle camouflage." The preserved viewport
screenshot in the development conversation is the human reference for that
failure; mesh-quality work remains open separately from this palette change.

A strict overhead categorical PNG established a provisional three-color
presentation and was accepted for runtime review:

- water: Agon palette 20, lighter azure `(0,85,255)` / RGBA2222 `0xF4`;
- NLCD developed classes 21 through 24: palette 7, light gray
  `(170,170,170)` / `0xEA`; and
- every remaining land class: palette 21, spring green `(0,170,85)` / `0xD8`.

The fifteen source material IDs remain unchanged for geometry and provenance;
only their fixture presentation collapses to those three bases. Every
full-value 4x4 base pattern is still one repeated native pixel. Normal scene
illumination remains enabled, so darker illumination bands may dither and are
not additional authored terrain classes.

The pattern compiler now canonicalizes machine-noise error to zero when an
exact native source such as palette 20 round-trips through RGB/HSV conversion.
The rebuilt library contains 45 unique illumination patterns, padded to the
fixed 126-pattern runtime ABI. All 387 host regressions pass. A silent emulator
qualification published and rendered the default 19 components with 38,319
color/depth pixels and shut down cleanly. Interactive presentation remains a
human validation gate, so these emulator-coupled changes remain uncommitted.

## Three-class natural terrain expanded to the complete regional corpus

The Author accepted the three-class presentation in the limited Berkeley
Marina runtime view and requested the same treatment for the entire corpus at
the previously accepted maximum view distance. The implementation applies both
independent distance controls already established by the project: the viewer
starts at manager ring five (91 resident components), and Pingo command 53 sets
the projection far distance to the maximum unsigned value `65535` immediately
after control creation. The projection value is about 524 km at the terrain
scale; it does not synthesize distant geometry or increase live residency.

The 91-cell v1 JSON bridge would scale to roughly 353 MB and several gigabytes
of transient Python objects. A new deterministic v2 bridge therefore extracts
the pinned accepted Blender master into three fixed little-endian sections:

- `VERTICES.bin`: 497,767 `<iiiI>` records / 7,964,272 bytes;
- `COMPONENTS.bin`: 50,183 `<II>` records / 401,464 bytes; and
- `FACES.bin`: 992,477 owner-grouped `<IIIIB>` records / 16,872,109 bytes.

The complete 25,237,845-byte bridge is rooted at
`build/source/bay-area-natural-post-partition-v2`. Its semantic SHA-256 is
`e9c8ad9059d17a6a3425fede36ea9c3935d8004b1cc50be9a40fe0c4ab5f4bf1`;
the three section hashes are recorded in `SOURCE.json`. Extraction remains
pinned to Blender SHA
`fd4dde2a9f366797808ef9bd395f0966386a8286aeca87b0c73c9ed4f1f64d02`
and topology/ownership SHA
`c7695f333e25812909fd2667614cb7213fee9de864dda417167181af128e5cca`.
Ordinary builds mmap and validate this bridge one component at a time without
requiring Blender.

The resulting `build/p3dm/bay-area-natural-full-l0` corpus has these exact
witnesses:

- 50,183 loose `NAT` records and 992,477 triangles;
- 842,903 component-local vertex instances;
- 25,251,544 container bytes and 20,178,854 unwrapped DATA bytes;
- 196 through 1,384 bytes per record, with no 16,384-byte loader overflow;

The 25.3 MB payload occupies about 235 MB on the host filesystem because each

worse on a FAT volume with large allocation units, so only the emulator proof
uses the complete loose tree. Indexed per-bucket AGNB bundles remain the
hardware deployment follow-up; source files and large receipts must not be
copied to an Agon SD card.

The regional viewer disables only the old Campanile experimental fence; true

## Simple distance LOD authorized

The Author found the roughly 2.30--3.30 km horizontal reach of ring five
insufficient to create a convincing sensation of flight and authorized the
first deliberately simple distance-LOD implementation. The saved Ring-5
Regional Terrain Streaming Prototype remains the rollback point. The new work
is a sibling experiment rather than a rewrite of that evidence.

The first control retains detailed near terrain through ring five, introduces
offline-decimated middle terrain over rings 6--15, and targets the simplest
six-triangle regular-hex representation over rings 16--30. All tiers retain
the accepted palette-20 water, palette-7 developed land, and palette-21 other
land presentation. Initial transitions may overlap and use skirts; proving
that distant real relief restores useful visual range takes priority over
solving the final conforming-seam problem. The ring-30 target should expose
roughly 15--20 km of terrain while adding a distant surface-triangle count of
the same order as the current detailed ring-five residency.

If this crude proof is visually successful, subsequent reduction must promote
the mathematical design already recorded for horizon silhouettes, ridges,
shorelines, categorical boundaries, projected error, and conforming seams.
Collision remains a separate query contract: the regular lattice permits
constant-time containing-triangle and barycentric-height tests without
intersecting the rendered triangle collection.

## Ring-30 distance control implemented

The first mixed-distance control is now a sibling of the saved ring-five
milestone. It leaves the accepted natural corpus untouched, keeps its 91 L0
objects through ring five, and adds the existing one-centre/six-triangle
regular control across the 2,700 cells in rings 6 through 30. The middle
designation covers rings 6--15 and the far designation covers rings 16--30;
both intentionally use the same crude geometry in this first visual test.

The annulus is packed into 92 generated P3DM records: 23 L1 chunks and 69 L2
chunks. It contains 13,910 component-local vertices, 16,200 surface triangles,
and 7,624 fail-soft skirt triangles. The containers occupy 384,488 bytes; the
largest is 5,600 bytes. Every selected cell occurs exactly once with six
surface faces. Chunk half-extents remain below the proven 1,792 m model extent,
giving Q8.8 scale 57,344. Together with the natural near field, the resident
control contains 183 objects, 14,840 vertices, 24,792 runtime triangles, and
416,692 loose-container bytes. The unchanged universal manager owns the whole
set and still performs at most one load per idle pass. Keys 0--5 vary the near
disk while all 92 distant chunks remain desired; total desired counts are
93, 99, 111, 129, 153, and 183.

The generated provider subtracts the current floating-origin displacement
from each coarse table transform, so natural and distant objects participate
in the same atomic manager rebase. Its full include closure has 52 reviewed
wide accesses and no definite or unresolved ADL-width hazards. The fixture is
40,340 bytes and uses the 65,535-unit projection far plane. A private headless
run with the committed no-object-limit Pingo VDP published, placed, and
activated all 183 objects without retry, loader, projection, or scene-capacity
errors. The converged renderer traversed all 183 objects (`ob=183`, `obt=183`),
the final frame populated all 76,800 depth pixels, and shutdown was clean.

The mandatory runtime-faithful review is
`build/visualization/bay-area-marina-r5-r30-hybrid-lod-review.blend`, with a
matching PNG and receipt. It decodes the actual P3DM records, separates the 92
skirts for inspection, uses only palette indices 20/7/21, and carries the exact
Marina camera. FSIM key `D` launches the control.

This proof is deliberately honest about its defects. The natural ring-five
boundary and ideal regular ring-six boundary do not conform, independently
quantized chunks can differ by about 0.14 m, the skirts cannot cure horizontal
gap/overlap or T-junctions, and the fixed ring-30 annulus does not follow an
aircraft that leaves the Marina. Human range and seam review decides whether
the cheap distant relief is useful enough to justify the real reducer.

## Mixed-topology distance control rejected

The Author's visual review rejected the natural-near/regular-far composition
as the active direction. Its nonconforming ownership boundary makes it too
difficult to distinguish useful distance reduction from artifacts introduced
only by mixing topology families. The fixture, generated corpus, Blender
review, receipts, and measurements remain reproducible negative evidence, but
the experiment leaves the authoritative TODO.

The replacement direction uses regular triangular lattices at every scale and
must cover the complete Bay Area corpus algorithmically. No boundary is to be
hand stitched. The already-qualified N=5 regular corpus remains the detailed
level. The first distance level is a strict N=1 decimation: each regular hex
retains its centre and six corners, all of which are vertices of the N=5
lattice. Full-region coarse chunks, runtime neighborhood tables, stable slot
mapping, transforms, skirts, and review artifacts are generated from the same
hex and projected-coordinate equations. A position-driven provider replaces
the Marina-static annulus.

An exhaustive alignment search selected a legal `8x4 -> 4x2 -> 2x1 -> 1x1`
hierarchy in `(q, offset-row)` space. A node that touches the detailed disk is
recursively replaced by its children; a node wholly outside that disk is kept
when it owns at least one required distance cell. Across all 32 possible
origin phases this covers every ring-6--30 cell exactly once, covers no
ring-0--5 cell, and selects 126--159 coarse objects. With the 91 detailed
objects, the maximum is 250, five below the existing manager's u8 limit.
Outer-only overfetch is allowed where a retained node crosses ring 30.

The four hierarchy levels contain 83,293 reusable records across the complete
region: 1,593 `8x4`, 6,372 `4x2`, 25,145 `2x1`, and 50,183 `1x1`. Guarded model
half-extents are respectively 2,001, 1,041, 1,024, and 1,024 metres, giving
legal Q8.8 scales 64,032, 33,312, 32,768, and 32,768. The leaf level preserves
the exact N=5 centre/corner encoded words. Larger nodes are independently
quantized and retain generated boundary skirts; they are a culling and range
control, not yet the final crack-free transition solution.
