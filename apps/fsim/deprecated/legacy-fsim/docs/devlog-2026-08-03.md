# Pingo Terrain development log — 2026-08-03

## Natural terrain before streaming partitions

The Author accepted a specific experimental inversion of the first hex
terrain pipeline. The compiler will first overbuild one natural, feature- and
error-driven surface. Only after triangulation will it partition existing
faces into bounded, roughly hexagonal streaming components. Logical axial
coordinates remain useful addresses and neighborhood seeds, but perfect
six-sided physical polygons no longer dictate terrain topology.

The durable provisional contract is
`docs/natural-terrain-post-partition-experiment.md`. It specifies protected
categorical, vector, DEM, airport, horizon, and corpus-boundary features;
adaptive refinement of angles, residuals, silhouettes, and plan-view feature
shape; coarse homogeneous interiors; deterministic overbuild supertiles;
mesh-edge partition routing near ideal logical hex boundaries; exact duplicated
seams; explicit physical adjacency and containment; collision and culling
bounds; and the still-open mixed-LOD problem.

The accepted straight-sided 50,183-cell N=5 corpus remains the runtime control.
The first experimental artifact will instead cover the complete pinned Bay
Area in Blender, retain the current material colors, preserve visible outer
edge effects, and use a distinct filename. It is a visual and structural
experiment only; it cannot promote the runtime format without the documented
determinism, topology, error, partition, manager, collision, LOD, emulator, and
physical-hardware gates.

## Complete-region natural-mesh evidence

The first full-region artifact now exists as
`build/visualization/bay-area-natural-post-partition.blend`, with a rendered
PNG and machine-readable `.review.json` receipt beside it. These generated
files remain ignored; `scripts/build_natural_region_blender.py`,
`src/pingo_terrain/natural_region.py`, and their tests are the durable build
path.

The provisional source analysis uses a globally phased 128 m grid over UTM
bounds E 521,600..624,640 m and N 4,099,968..4,229,376 m. It samples 815,672
positions from the checksum-pinned 3DEP and NLCD sources. Numerical DEM
residual and discrete-Hessian tests, water-transition points, protected water
segments, the corpus edge, and one anchor for every logical address reduce
that analysis to 499,681 constrained-Delaunay inputs. This first profile is
deliberately water-first. It does not claim the complete vector/stream/ridge
feature graph described by the design.

Blender produces 497,767 retained vertices and 992,477 visible triangles. Six
unseeded faces at the permitted outer clip are omitted; 992,477 retained faces
are each owned exactly once. Every one of the 50,183 logical addresses is
nonempty and connected, with 1..76 faces (mean 19.777) and a maximum component
vertex extent of 972.470 m, below the existing 1,024 m encoding half-extent.
The protected water graph contains 29,075 source segments: 28,999 survive as
one edge and 76 survive as exact two-edge collinear chains.

A useful failed control preceded the accepted partition. Assigning every
finished face solely from its ideal-hex centroid left 157 logical addresses
empty and split 1,741 owners into disconnected islands. The artifact therefore
uses one adjacency-safe locked source face per address, an ideal-owner-first
physical-distance flood over the completed mesh's dual graph, and a connected
repair for the small rejected regions. This is genuine post-mesh partitioning:
no terrain face is sliced to manufacture a component boundary.

The experiment also identifies an unresolved contract choice. Its explicit
physical graph has 128,259 adjacencies, including 3,106 noncanonical contacts,
and omits edge contact for 24,500 ideal logical-neighbor pairs. Components are
still complete, connected, bounded, and explicitly identified, which satisfies
this accepted exploratory proposal, but the graph is not equivalent to the
canonical six-neighbor lattice. Runtime promotion must either constrain the
partition skeleton to that combinatorics or qualify the explicit-graph manager
contract. The 0.180-degree minimum triangle angle likewise shows that protected
constraint/quality repair remains necessary before runtime emission.

## Accepted first runtime fixture for the natural mesh

The complete Blender artifact is accepted as the authoritative offline source
for the next experiment, but it is not itself ready to load into Pingo. The
next implementation step is a deliberately bounded runtime proof rather than
an attempted full-corpus promotion.

The first fixture will adapt the proven direct free-camera viewer and use one
modest regional subset at one uniform near LOD. Its purpose is to qualify the
new boundary between offline natural terrain and the universal runtime terrain
manager:

1. emit independently loadable P3DM terrain components from the completed
   master surface without slicing new geometrically regular boundaries into
   it;
2. duplicate and validate every same-LOD seam after final P3DM quantization;
3. package the bounded component family in the existing indexed/containerized
   loading scheme;
4. publish and retire components through the authoritative universal terrain
   manager rather than introducing fixture-specific loading logic;
5. expose enough free-camera motion to cross several irregular component
   boundaries and make missing faces, cracks, bad bounds, publication errors,
   or transform errors obvious; and
6. measure object, vertex, triangle, encoded-byte, load/publication, and frame
   behavior before choosing a wider corpus or mixed-distance LOD policy.

This fixture intentionally defers mixed LOD, distant terrain, collision,
airports, and full-region streaming. It must use distinct generated filenames
and fixture identity, preserving the accepted straight-sided N=5 corpus and
viewer as controls. Success means a reproducible bounded corpus, exact
same-LOD seams, clean manager lifecycle, and a visually usable emulator proof;
it does not decide whether a later regional provider will retain the explicit
physical adjacency graph or constrain partitions to canonical six-neighbor
combinatorics.

### Diagnostic color and illumination control

The Author added a presentation constraint for this first runtime fixture.
Each of the fifteen authored terrain base colors will be mapped to one exact
opaque Agon RGBA2222 palette color. The neutral/base entry for every material
will be a solid repeated pixel, with no spatial dithering. Terrain remains
scene-illuminated, however, and lower illumination/value levels may use the
normal flat-pattern value approximation. Consequently any pattern visible on
a face is attributable to the illumination multiplier selecting a non-base
value, rather than dithering already present in the material's original color.

This is a diagnostic fixture-specific material profile. It does not replace
the accepted richer source-color vocabulary or the existing dithered Berkeley
control. The build receipt must publish the exact RGB8-to-RGBA2222 mapping and
prove that every base 4x4 pattern contains one repeated opaque palette byte.

## First natural-terrain runtime implementation

The bounded runtime proof now extracts the complete Campanile-centred
radius-five disk from the accepted Blender master. The portable `SOURCE.json`
bridge is pinned to Blender artifact SHA-256
`fd4dde2a9f366797808ef9bd395f0966386a8286aeca87b0c73c9ed4f1f64d02`,
topology/ownership SHA-256
`c7695f333e25812909fd2667614cb7213fee9de864dda417167181af128e5cca`,
and semantic extraction SHA-256
`10758c3e4633e199af85c2829af3313923220db65267ec999e033d780227be88`.
It reads projected point and owner/material face attributes, never Blender's
scaled display coordinates.

The compiler emits 91 loose `NAT` P3DM records containing 1,461
component-local vertex instances and 1,699 triangles. Their DATA payloads total
34,978 bytes and complete AGNB containers total 44,156 bytes; individual files
are 196..772 bytes, far below the 16,384-byte loader ceiling. Components retain
their ideal axial centres and common Pingo scale, but contain only the finished
natural-master surface. There are no skirts. Exact reconstruction after P3DM
encoding proves 449 selected-to-selected seam edges across 205 physical
component pairs, 122 multi-owner boundary vertices with at most six owners,
and zero ideal-world integer mismatches. An additional model of the VDP's
actual binary32 position conversion, object scale, command-52 translation, and
final multiply/add finds that 144 of 389 shared vertices are not literally
bit-equal between owners; all differences are in X and the maximum span is
only 0.000030517578125 Pingo unit. This bound is now part of the generated
receipt so the mathematical seam proof is not mistaken for a float-bit claim.

The existing topology-neutral manager is unchanged. A compile-time provider
profile changes only the record namespace to `NAT` and bounds candidate cells
to axial distance five from `(90,121)`. The same free-camera survey starts at
the established Campanile pose, defaults to a 19-component radius-two disk,
and accepts live radius keys 0 through 5. FSIM keeps the regular control at
entry `0` and exposes this sibling with `N`.

For the Author's diagnostic, all fifteen semantic base materials now resolve
to exact opaque RGBA2222 pixels. Closely related classes deliberately share
native colors: water is `(85,85,170)`; developed land is `(170,170,170)` or
`(85,85,85)`; barren/grass/crops are `(170,170,85)`; deciduous/pasture are
`(85,170,85)`; evergreen/mixed forest are `(0,85,0)`; shrub is `(85,85,0)`;
and wetlands are `(0,85,85)` or `(85,170,170)`. Every full-value 4x4 base
pattern is one repeated palette byte. Normal scene illumination remains
enabled with the established east light and 75 percent ambient; darker value
bands may dither. Visible patterning is therefore evidence of illumination,
not an already-dithered source color. `PATTERN.json` records every semantic
mapping, packed byte, pattern ID, and resource checksum.
