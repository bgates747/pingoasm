## Transition qualification remains open

The cold result proves only one complete plan. It does not qualify a cell
crossing, phase change, radius change, or regional-edge traversal. In
particular, parent/child replacement is not group-atomic: the manager retires
stale active owners before publishing replacement leaves one per idle pass.
This avoids deliberate parent/child overlap but can expose a temporary hole
while a multi-record replacement converges. The generated outer skirts are a
fail-soft crack mask, not a conforming mixed-LOD seam, and independently
quantized L8/L4 neighbors can still reveal same-level discrepancies.

Host/exhaustive modeling passes all 6,912 adjacent phase/radius transitions
and 156,347 boundary candidates with 8,035 retained nodes; an actual live
transition capture remains open.

The next gate is therefore a deterministic moving-origin transition capture
that exercises protected-slot retention, parent-to-children and
children-to-parent changes, keys 0 through 5, corpus edges, and convergence
after every change. Human seam/pop review and physical-hardware performance
remain mandatory. Until those tests pass, this is the preferred regular-only
experiment, not a promoted crack-free terrain solution.
