# Development log — 2026-08-02

## Lara motion-capture hardware acceptance

The first complete application of external motion capture to the existing
rigid-part Lara model passed physical Agon hardware review. The accepted build
loops the conditioned first stride of Bandai Namco Research Motiondataset 2
`run_normal_001`, renders the corrected body-part and texture mapping, and
retains application-owned movement, look-at camera, pause, and reset controls.

The qualified `apps/anim` payload was deployed flat to
`/PingoWolf/Lara/` on the AGON SD card after clearing that leaf directory:

| File | Bytes | SHA-256 |
|---|---:|---|
| `anim.bin` | 29,591 | `268e5824c3c9cde4bb7b2471c8a47559f03e6a384b43579c71be7eead2bfba69` |
| `Lara.rgba2` | 47,104 | `839f6331f8d4c53078542c3a6a6a99518d6ac4f0515e874b74ae9e1d91b37700` |

Deployment deliberately left `autoexec.txt` under Author control. Both files
were verified byte-for-byte by SHA-256, the card was flushed and safely
unmounted, and the Author subsequently reported that the hardware test passed.
This acceptance covers the behavior and rendering of that exact build; it does
not alter the Bandai Namco CC BY-NC 4.0 requirements or resolve the separately
documented Lara model provenance.
